Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Internshala Company Blocker helps users hide internship and job listings from companies they choose on Internshala.

Data stored locally

The extension stores a user’s blocked-company list in Chrome’s local extension storage on that user’s device.

Website content

While a user visits Internshala, the extension reads company names displayed in listing cards only to determine whether a card matches the local blocklist. It then hides matching cards in that browser tab.

No data transfer

The extension does not send, sell, share, upload, or transmit the blocklist, Internshala page content, browsing activity, or personal information to any server or third party.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support address listed on the Chrome Web Store page.
